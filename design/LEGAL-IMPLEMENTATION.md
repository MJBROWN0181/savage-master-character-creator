## Remaining operator work

- Have a Texas attorney review operator identity, the liability cap, indemnity, governing-law language, account assent, and privacy disclosures before treating these terms as legally vetted. These pages reduce ambiguity; they do not confer publisher permissions or guarantee immunity from claims.
- `support@smsheets.com` is a general copyright-reporting contact. No DMCA designated-agent registration or safe-harbor eligibility was established by this implementation. If seeking Section 512 safe harbor for hosted user material, register a designated agent, publish all required agent contact details (including address and telephone), maintain and enforce a repeat-infringer policy, accommodate standard technical measures, and operate compliant notices/counter-notices. Registration needs business details not yet supplied here.
- Monitor the copyright inbox and support tickets. Preserve reports; locate the disputed upload/post; restrict substantiated infringement through the appropriate administrative controls; notify affected users; get counsel involved for disputed statutory notices and counter-notices. Do not claim automatic takedown or restoration is implemented.
- Confirm the real business/legal identity and public contact information if the trading name changes. Recheck data processing, retention, payment disclosures, and any added tracking against the privacy notice when features change.
- Publisher credits do not clear all existing setting content or images. Keep source-level licensing review separate from these terms. ORC notices do not license Reserved Material.
