# Fifth-edition character workspace

Uses the CC-BY-4.0 SRD 5.2.1 (revised 2024 rules), not the 2014 rules or paid books. Required attribution remains on the page and printed sheets. No official art or logos.

Five steps cover identity, abilities, training, equipment/magic, and a printable sheet. Browser drafts use a separate key; account snapshots use `system: dnd5e`, `setting: dnd5e`, and `rules: srd-5.2.1`. Existing account ownership checks, campaigns, and profile favorites support both systems. The Savage Worlds loader excludes 5e saves.

Calculated: background increases, modifiers, proficiency by level, class saving throws, selected skill proficiency/expertise, Criminal Alert initiative, basic unarmored AC, level-one HP (including Dwarf), passive Perception, and class spell attack/DC.

Recorded by the player with SRD guidance: class/species/feat choices, legal skill counts, species lineage, tools and languages, starting equipment, attacks, prepared spells/slots, features, later-level HP and defenses. Multiclass automation, leveling features, conditional bonuses (such as Jack of All Trades), and an integrated spell/equipment catalogue are not implemented. GM-approved physical rolls are entered manually. The interface states these limits so calculated numbers are not presented as comprehensive rules validation.

Backups are shape/size/identifier checked. Cloud saves also validate name, allocations, and explicit later-level HP. Tests exercise math, invalid allocations, malformed files, owner-only account access, and separation from the existing system.
